The problem

AI content all sounds the same.
Because it all starts the same way: writing.

✕ GENERIC

No point of view

Prompt-to-post tools don't know what your founder believes, so they fill the gap with the average opinion of the internet.

✕ UNGROUNDED

No proof

Claims without customer stories, numbers, or specifics read as filler — because they are. Your audience can tell.

✕ INTERCHANGEABLE

Anyone could have posted it

If a competitor could publish the same post by swapping the logo, it builds their category — not your company.

SignalStory inverts the process: writing is the last step, not the first. Every draft is grounded in what your company believes, says, and can prove — before a single sentence is written.

How it works

From signal to publish-ready in four moves.

Capture the signal

Submit wins by hand, or connect Pipedrive, Attio, Linear, and GitHub — deals, launches, and milestones flow in automatically. Anything else pipes in through Zapier or Make.

Ground it in your company

Every signal is matched against your founder's beliefs, brand voice, editorial strategy — and your knowledge library, cited like [S1] so claims come with receipts.

Think first, write last

Five agents score, angle, and structure the story before a single writing agent turns the brief into channel-native drafts.

Review drafts with receipts

Publish-ready LinkedIn, X, and blog drafts land in your dashboard with the proof behind every claim. Approve, tweak, or regenerate a single channel.

Under the hood

Six agents. Two quality gates. One writer.

Most tools ask one model to do everything at once. SignalStory splits the job into specialists — and puts a gate before the writing and a gate after it.

1

Event Listener

extraction

Turns the raw signal into clean, verifiable facts — what happened, who was involved, what can be proven.

2

Significance Scorer

reasoning

Judges whether this signal deserves content at all, scored against your editorial strategy.

Gate 1 · Significance

Not everything deserves a post.

Weak signals are rejected here — with a note on what would change the verdict — instead of becoming forced, thin content.

significant → continues weak → rejected, with reasons
3

Story Finder

reasoning

Generates competing story angles — the different lenses this signal could be told through — and ranks them for distinctiveness.

4

Narrative Strategist

reasoning

Commits to one angle and builds the brief: the argument, the structure, and a ledger of claims — each marked supported or unsupported by your knowledge library.

5

Channel Transformer

writing

The first and only agent that writes prose. It turns the brief into a LinkedIn post, an X thread, and a blog piece — each shaped for how that channel is actually read.

6

Anti-Slop Editor

reasoning

The adversarial last line. It scores every draft on one brutal question — could a generic AI have written this without your context?

Gate 2 · Anti-slop

Generic drafts don't ship.

A failing draft gets exactly one rewrite with the editor's notes. Still generic? It's flagged for your attention instead of quietly published.

passes → ready for review fails once → one rewrite fails twice → flagged for you
